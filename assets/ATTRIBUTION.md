# Asset attribution

Preserved project and vendor marks come from Devicon, Simple Icons, CNCF artwork, and official repositories. Original source records are included in docs/DEVOPS-ASSET-SOURCES.json and docs/KUBERNETES-ASSET-SOURCES.json. Marks belong to their owners and do not imply endorsement.

Covers, galleries, badges, and diagrams are original repository-native vector layouts. Resource cards are conceptual labels, not official resource logos. The official VERIQTA symbol was unavailable, so assets use plain VERIQTA text.
